Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Compliant with Google Play Developer Policies

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy governs the use of Vektra PDF: Reader & Sign ("we", "our", or "the app"). We believe that your documents, contracts, notes, and signatures belong exclusively to you. The app is engineered with an Offline-First architecture: all file parsing, rendering, document signing, compression, and image conversion are executed locally on your device hardware without transmitting user files to any cloud servers.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Personal Data: We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

Document Content: Your PDF files, scanned images, and generated signatures remain strictly on your local device storage. We have no access to your documents or their contents.

3. Permissions & Device Access

Vektra PDF requests only the minimal system permissions required to provide its core functionalities:

  • Storage / Media Files (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Strictly required to discover, open, display, and save PDF files, images, and office documents upon user request.
  • Camera Access (ML Kit Document Scanner): Used in real-time when the user activates the document scanner to capture document pages. Camera frames are processed on-device and never stored or uploaded externally.
  • Internet & Network State: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google Play In-App Review API to fetch ads and facilitate ratings.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To keep the application free for all users worldwide, we utilize Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID, approximate IP-derived location, and app performance telemetry) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

• Learn more about Google's data handling: Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising

Users who upgrade to Vektra PDF PRO enjoy an entirely ad-free experience, where all advertising SDK requests are permanently suppressed.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal requirements. Any modifications will be published directly on this URL with an updated effective date.

7. Developer Contact

If you have any inquiries, suggestions, or concerns regarding your privacy or data protection while using Vektra PDF, please reach out via our Google Play Console developer profile.
