Privacy Policy for Vektra PDF: Reader & Sign

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vektra PDF: Reader & Sign ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user data and permissions.

1. Data Collection & Privacy

Vektra PDF is built with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal files, documents, photos, or digital signatures to any external servers. All PDF rendering, image conversions, and document signing happen entirely offline on your local device.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media Files: Required strictly to scan and display PDF documents located on your device and to save converted PDFs and signatures to your storage.
  • Internet & Network State: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display relevant advertisements.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID, IP address, and device diagnostics) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's policies and applicable privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA).

For more details on how Google uses data, visit: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via Google Play Console support.
